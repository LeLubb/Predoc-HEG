# Predoc-HEG

This repo will help me and my supervisor to monitor my work at HEG - Genève (part of HES-SO). It is mainly about energy economics.

A first work study seasonality in wind speeds across Switzerland. A second work aims to produce descriptive data for wind speed and solar radiation on two timescale: 1980-2025 and 2015-2025, depending on elevation, geographic zone and time period. A third (ongoing) work is dedicated to the production of a paper studying complementarity in Switzerland between wind and solar resources using meteorological data - involving a literature review and graphs propduction beside usual papers parts.
